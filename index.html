🔔 Weekly Plan slot alerts

This page helps you enable or remove optional Weekly Plan browser notifications. Enabling them stores your browser push subscription in the company's Google Apps Script project, linked to your signed-in work account, after you paste the code into the portal. The subscription contains a push endpoint and encryption keys. The browser's push provider processes delivery metadata. Notification content includes your current and next 30-minute planned activities and may be visible on your device lock screen.
